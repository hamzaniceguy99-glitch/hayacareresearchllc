Program 01 · Saying no to the wrong protocol

Study feasibility & start-up

Sites that accept protocols they cannot recruit for waste a sponsor's money and participants' time. We assess honestly whether the population, the schedule and our capability fit the protocol, and decline when they do not.

  • Sponsors and CROs

Program 02 · Criteria exist for a reason

Recruitment & screening

Eligibility criteria protect participants, not just data. We screen carefully and tell people plainly when a study is not right for them, because enrolling someone who does not fit puts them at risk and compromises the study for everyone else.

  • Per study
  • Potential participants
  • No cost to participants

Program 03 · Take the form home

Informed consent & study visits

Consent is a discussion, not a signature. You can take the form away, discuss it with your own physician and family, and ask anything. You may withdraw at any time, for any reason, and it will not affect the care you receive anywhere else.

  • Per protocol
  • Participants
  • No cost to participants
  • Written consent

Program 04 · Ready for a monitor

Data & regulatory documentation

Research is only worth anything if the data is accurate and traceable. We keep source documentation contemporaneous, respond to monitoring queries promptly, and maintain a regulatory file that stands up to an audit rather than one assembled before a visit.
